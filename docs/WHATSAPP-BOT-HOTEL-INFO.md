# WhatsApp booking bot: hotel information (ready to paste)

Each hotel's bot has its own answers, written from that hotel's own knowledge base (the guest-safe docs `nap` and `offers-and-policies` only; never the
marketing docs). Paste each topic into the Booking bot card: Dashboard → WhatsApp → Automation → pick the number → "Hotel information" → Add a topic.
The title is a list row (max 24 characters). Keep facts that rarely change; dated offers are left out (guests see live prices and offers in the room list).
Tax is written as each website shows it (checked 17 Nov 2026). If a website's tax or a policy changes, change the topic.

Card settings per hotel:
- **Elegant:** hotel name "Hotel Elegant Executive Suites"; advance: percent of the total (set the percent; Shoaib 17 Nov: advance is taken on WhatsApp bookings, regular rate too); hold 120 minutes;
  payment text with the account details; **refund note: "If you cannot make it, cancel any time before check-in and get a 100% refund."** (The refund is for a stay that did not happen, not for one the guest has already stayed.)
  (A special offer that needs advance payment is always charged in full in advance.)
- **Silver Sand:** hotel name "Hotel Silver Sand Multan"; advance: **None: pay at the hotel**. (Silver Sand has no advance-payment flow, per its KB.) Refund note can stay empty.

## Hotel Silver Sand Multan

**Location & contact**
Hotel Silver Sand Multan, 514 Akbar Road, Railway Colony, Multan 60000. Open 24 hours. Call or WhatsApp: 0300 8720939. Landline: 061 4503355. Map: https://maps.google.com/?q=30.1823799,71.4422918. **Check-in & check-out**
Check-in: 24 hours, any time. Check-out: 12:00 to 1:00 pm.

**Facilities**
Breakfast included, free private parking, room service (no restaurant), and airport pick-up and drop on request (charged).

**Policies**
Children of all ages are welcome; from age 3 they are counted as adults. Extra bed: PKR 1,000 per child (0 to 12 years) per night. No cots. No pets. No parties.

**Payment & cancellation**
Rates are shown before tax; 16% GST is added on top. Pay at the hotel in cash. No advance payment is needed. If you cannot make it, you can cancel any time before check-in and get a full refund.
