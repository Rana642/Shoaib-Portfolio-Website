## The challenge

Nearly every booking arrived through OTAs — with 15–20% commission attached. The hotel
had no functioning pixel, no remarketing, and a booking page that took eight seconds to
load on mobile.

## What I did

- Wired tracking first: Meta Pixel + Conversions API, GA4 goals on the booking engine
- Rebuilt the funnel: fast landing pages per room category, one clear booking action
- Meta campaigns split by intent: destination reach, warm remarketing, past-guest lists
- Google Search protecting the brand name from OTA bidding

## The results

Within 90 days, direct bookings — the commission-free kind — were up 300% against the
prior quarter, and the property could finally see which spend produced which stays.
