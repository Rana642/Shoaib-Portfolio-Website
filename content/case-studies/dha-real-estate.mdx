## The challenge

The team was drowning in leads that never picked up the phone. Volume looked great in
the ad manager; the sales floor told a different story.

## What I did

- Rebuilt lead forms with qualifying friction — budget range, timeline, payment plan
- Creative that pre-sold the price bracket instead of hiding it
- Conversions API wired to the CRM so ads optimized toward *contacted* leads, not raw form fills
- Weekly feedback loop with the sales team on lead quality

## The results

Cost per **qualified** lead — the number that actually pays commissions — landed at
roughly half the market benchmark, and the sales team stopped ignoring the ad leads.
