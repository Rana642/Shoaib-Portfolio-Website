## The challenge

People searching for legal help in Multan were finding competitors first — and the firm's
contact page asked for everything short of a case file before showing a phone number.

## What I did

- Google Search campaigns per practice area, matched to how clients actually search
- Negative keyword discipline to keep unqualified matters out of the spend
- A simplified intake: call button first, short form second
- Call tracking so consultations, not clicks, measured the account

## The results

Consultation bookings grew quarter over quarter, with the spend concentrated on the two
practice areas that produced the firm's best cases.
