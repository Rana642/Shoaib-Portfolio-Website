## The challenge

A footwear brand starting from zero: no pixel seasoning, no purchase history, and two
previous attempts at ads that burned budget with nothing to show.

## What I did

- Tracking before spend: pixel, CAPI, and GA4 verified against test purchases
- A creative testing matrix — six angles, three formats, disciplined kill criteria
- Campaign structure built for learning speed at a small budget
- Scaled only the angles that held ROAS across two consecutive weeks

## The results

The account crossed into profitable ROAS within 60 days of the first ad — and the
winning creative angles became the brand's organic content playbook too.
