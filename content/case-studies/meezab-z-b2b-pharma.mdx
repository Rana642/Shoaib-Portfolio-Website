## The challenge

A B2B pharma manufacturer whose entire pipeline depended on trade exhibitions — expensive,
seasonal, and impossible to scale between events.

## What I did

- Positioned the catalog for distributors, not consumers — the targeting followed
- LinkedIn-style intent on Meta: job-role creative with a distributor-focused offer
- A lightweight landing page with a qualification form and WhatsApp handoff
- Remarketing sequences for the long B2B consideration cycle

## The results

Distributor inquiries went from twice-a-year bursts to a consistent weekly flow the sales
team could actually plan around.
